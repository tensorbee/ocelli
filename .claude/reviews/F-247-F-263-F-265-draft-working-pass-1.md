The fresh model report's two proposed challenges are unsupported and contradicted by the primary standard. Its first claim would introduce a wrong frame-boundary rule into the draft. PS3.5 section 8.1.2 describes concatenated overlay frames with padding only at the complete Value Field. PS3.3 C.9.3 independently confirms no frame delimiters or padding between overlay frames. F247's contiguous per-frame bit-offset control is correct. [PS3.5 chapter 8](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part05/chapter_8.html), [Multi-frame Overlay Module](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part03/sect_C.9.3.html).

The model's F265 claim that Waveform Bits Stored does not exist is also false. It is (003A,021A), Type 1, and has its own attribute description. The generic module also lists 8,16,32 and 64 allocated bits with corresponding sample interpretations, subject to IOD constraints. F265 deliberately proposes a bounded 16-bit subset and defers exact IOD/transcription choices. Challenging stored and allocated bits does not itself widen that subset. Actual Channel Time Skew and Channel Offset are (003A,0214) and (003A,0218). The model's 5400,1012/1014 citations are wrong. [Waveform Module](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part03/sect_C.10.9.html).

The model's shutter references are also inaccurate. Geometric shutter intersection is supported by C.7.6.11. Bitmap shutter set bits are replaced by the shutter presentation value, and that plane is not activated as a conventional overlay, under C.7.6.15. C.10.5 is Graphic Annotation. The proposed F247 controls preserve the correct behavior while leaving presentation choices open. [Display Shutter Module](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part03/sect_C.7.6.11.html), [Bitmap Display Shutter Module](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part03/sect_C.7.6.15.html).
