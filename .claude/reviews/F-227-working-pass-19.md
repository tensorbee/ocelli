## Defects

### D1, "freed inside that job" and "live only inside F-216's permit term" are false when the samples are parked

**Where**: `examples/folder-viewer/src/series_window.rs:12-15`, "its decoded samples and their unpacked copy live only inside F-216's permit term and are freed inside that job". `docs/lld/pixel-pipeline.md:256-258`, "the decoded samples and their unpacked copy are inside F-216's permit term and are freed inside that job", which the next sentence at :259 contradicts.
**What**: the job hands the samples to `recycle_sample_buffer` (`serve.rs`, `WindowJob::run`), which parks them on the free list whenever cached and free bytes fit the budget. A parked buffer outlives the job and is charged to `OCELLI_CACHE_MIB`, not to the permit term. Only the unpacked copy is freed inside the job every time.
**Why it is wrong**: microscope section 3, a false sentence in an LLD and a module doc. The round test asserts the opposite of the sentence: with room to spare it requires 8,192 bytes parked after the round.
**Evidence**: four parallel rounds at 172 MiB, `free` went from 2,097,152 to 4,194,304. One round at 64 MiB, `free` 2,097,152 to 4,194,304 after the disconnect rounds. The round test's room case.
**Scope and regression**: prose, present on HEAD. Fix: "the decoded samples and their unpacked copy exist only inside that job, under its permit. The job frees the unpacked copy, and parks the samples on the free list, inside `OCELLI_CACHE_MIB`, when cached and free bytes fit the budget, and frees them otherwise." Say it once in each file and delete the contradicted clause rather than adding a qualifier.

## Smells

### S1, the pool's reservation is outside every term of F-216's bound and no doc names it

**Where**: `crates/ocelli-pixel/src/auto_window.rs:217-230` (unchanged, `Vec::with_capacity(sample_budget(frames) * frames)`), held across the round by `series_window.rs:139-170`. Bound: `.claude/plans/F-216-design.md:279`. `docs/lld/cache.md:196`, "One more pool sits outside the budget", which names only the kept inspect replies.
**What**: each round in flight reserves up to 4 MiB when it starts and holds it until it ends, on the connection thread between jobs and inside each job. It is not charged to the cache and `GET /held` does not report it. The page asks for every shown series at once, one round per series, up to `MAX_SIDE * MAX_SIDE` = 9 cells. Chromium's six connections a host would cap one page at six rounds, 24 MiB, which is arithmetic and not measured.
**Failure mechanism**: the ruling's "F-216's bound needs no new term" holds only if the 32 MiB constant, defined as the binary, stacks and slack, is read as also covering 16 MiB, measured, for four cells. `viewer.resident_memory` opens no series without a window, so a growth in that reservation (a larger `AUTO_WINDOW_SAMPLE_CAP`, or more cells) is invisible to the only runner that checks the bound. The reservation predates this range. The new prose makes it newly relevant, because it now describes where every other part of a round's memory lives.
**Disposition**: the lead rules, since it decides the bound. Options: (1) name it, in cache.md beside the kept pool and in pixel-pipeline.md, as "each round in flight reserves its pool, at most 4 MiB, outside `OCELLI_CACHE_MIB`, one per series the page asks for, 16,777,216 bytes measured for four", and state that the 32 MiB constant is taken to cover it. (2) Add a term of 4 MiB per concurrent round and add the `pet_no_window` workload to `viewer.resident_memory`.
