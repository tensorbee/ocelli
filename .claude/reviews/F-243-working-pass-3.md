## Defects

### D1, four sentences say every undamaged stream of the generated corpus decodes to CharLS's samples, and one does not decode

**Where**: `vendor/ritk-codecs-0.6.0/PATCH-PROVENANCE.md:27` to `:29`, "Every undamaged stream of F-243's generated corpus and of review pass 2's 6,724 decodes with the patch to the samples CharLS 2.4.4 gives". `.claude/plans/F-243-design.md:247` (AC21) and `:327` (the parity table), "Every undamaged stream decodes to CharLS's samples". `docs/lld/codecs.md:113` to `:114`, "since every undamaged stream decodes to its samples". `crates/ocelli-codec/tests/jpegls.rs:1234`, "Every undamaged stream must decode to CharLS's samples", which the same test contradicts at `:1292` with "except the hand-built one-padded line".
**What**: the generated corpus labels `jpegls_final_ff_one_padded_u8` with the class `undamaged`, and CharLS and Ocelli both refuse it, measured. So it decodes to no samples at all. `docs/lld/codecs.md:138` and `use.html:631` say this correctly ("that CharLS opens, 149 of 150"). The four sentences above do not.
**Why it is wrong**: a claim in prose that is false, `.claude/commands/microscope.md` section 3. Pass 2's D4 found this same fault in `use.html`. The remediation corrected that file and wrote the unqualified claim again in `PATCH-PROVENANCE.md` and the plan.
**Evidence**: `cmp_gc.py` over my harness's decode of the 9,259 cases lists the undamaged streams CharLS refuses: only `jpegls_final_ff_one_padded_u8`. My harness refuses it as `DecoderFailure`.
**Scope and regression**: prose only, no pixel. The smallest fix is "every undamaged stream that CharLS opens", as the LLD's later paragraph says. `PATCH-PROVENANCE.md` is hash-pinned, so `RITK_PROVENANCE_SHA256` moves with it.

## Nitpicks

- **N1.** 0xFF fill before SOF or SOS, which T.81 B.1.1.2 also allows, is refused on 3,000 of 3,000 cases where CharLS opens to the stream's own samples. One or two 0xFF after EOI are refused as `TrailingData` on 2,000 cases of the fill sweep and 1,053 of the damaged sweep where CharLS opens, the adapter's existing rule. The ruling covers fill before EOI only, these are refusals on the safe side, and no document claims otherwise. Recorded so the next pass does not count them as missed.
- **N2.** `docs/lld/codecs.md:152`, "136 with one or two 0x00 bytes inserted before EOI". All 136 are `ins:00`, one byte. Every `ins:0000` case agrees with CharLS. The lead's ruling says "one inserted 0x00", which is the exact form.
- **N3.** `tests/jpegls.rs:1349`, "CharLS 2.4.4 opens both, recorded in the generated corpus". The test's frame, `jpegls_two_band_u16_32x32.jls`, is not one of the corpus's 150 streams. CharLS does open it with one and two fill bytes, to its undamaged digest, measured now. The corpus records fill bytes on 150 other streams.
- **N4.** `bitstream.rs:128` to `:129`, "CharLS 2.4.4 was measured to keep counting zeros and open such a scan instead". What was measured is that CharLS opens such a scan to the sample the unextended code gives. "Keep counting zeros" is a reading of CharLS's internals, which this review does not open.
- **N5.** The 6,724 cited in `PATCH-PROVENANCE.md`, `docs/lld/codecs.md` and `use.html` rest on pass 2's scratch streams, which were deleted, so the figure is not reproducible from the tree. This pass's 62,739 support the claim, and it is not false.
- **N6, carried from pass 2 N2.** The limited-length prefix refusal still sets the `exhausted` flag, so it reports "ran out of entropy-coded data" for a scan that did not run out. Nothing a caller sees changes.
- **N7, carried from pass 2 N3.** `generate_jpegls.py` still records CharLS's undamaged digest as the reference without asserting it against the image. This pass asserted it for its own streams.
- **N8, carried from pass 2 N5.** Plan item 8 still says imagecodecs "cannot write P 12". `bitspersample` wrote every P from 2 to 16 in this pass.
